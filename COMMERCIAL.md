Relay is released under the **PolyForm Noncommercial License 1.0.0** (see [`LICENSE.md`](./LICENSE.md)).
That license lets individuals, gaming clans, hobbyists, students, and noncommercial
organizations use, modify, self-host, and share Relay **for free**, forever, for any
noncommercial purpose.

**Commercial use requires a separate paid license from the copyright holder.**

You need a commercial license if you want to do any of the following with Relay
(or a modified version of it):

- Sell it, resell it, or sell access to it.
- Offer it as a paid or revenue-generating hosted/SaaS service.
- Bundle it into a paid product or a commercial offering.
- Use it internally to run or support a for-profit business.

If you are unsure whether your use is commercial, assume it is and get in touch —
it is usually quick and inexpensive to sort out.

## Get a commercial license

Contact the copyright holder:

- **Licensor:** Liquid ICT

Send commercial-licensing enquiries through the website above.

Please include: your name/company, what you want to build or offer, and roughly the
scale (users, revenue, deployment). Terms are negotiated case by case.
